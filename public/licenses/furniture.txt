Modifications by designon: models were uniformly scaled to catalogue dimensions,
centered on their footprints and seated on y=0. Geometry was compressed with
Meshopt and textures with WebP. These are adaptations of the upstream models;
original copyright metadata is retained. The runtime omits imported lights and
cameras so furniture does not change the building's selected solar lighting.
Original source modifications described in the linked READMEs are retained.

# MSH pass — the chrome of the 3D view

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Subject:** the Spatial (3D) view, the Renders gallery and the phone's tool dock.
**Method:** the real interface, measured in Chromium at 390 × 844, 1024 × 800 and
1440 × 900. Every number below is measured from the rendered DOM, not read from
the stylesheet, and each was taken before and after the change on the same
machine and the same build pipeline.
**Outcome:** the bottom of the 3D view is one surface instead of five bands, the
3D toolbar on a phone is one row instead of five, and the model owns the screen
at every width it is measured at.

---

## Found

### 1. The bottom of the 3D view was five bands — 1440, 1024, 390

Measured below the 3D model, before this change:

| viewport | the model | the bottom | the frame around it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1440 × 900 | 557 px | **255 px** | 303 px (33.7 % of the viewport) |
| 1024 × 800 | 355 px | **285 px** | 333 px (41.6 %) |
| 390 × 844 | 270 px | **333 px** | 526 px (62.3 %) |

Five bands, each with its own rule, each holding a handful of small things and a
large amount of nothing:

| band | height | what it held |
|---|---|---|
| furniture editing | 50 px | one 119 px dropdown, then 1193 px of empty sheet |
| study info | 52 px | two buttons, a three-line readout, and a sentence, with voids between them |
| the numbers | 60 px | five cells of equal weight, the first of which was a heading |
| the time controls | 45 px | a scrubber stretched to ~800 px for a 24-hour range |
| the assistant | 48 px | the coach |

**The cost.** A user looking at a building in 3D was looking at 62 % chrome on a
phone. The model's own rectangle had 270 px of an 844 px screen.

### 2. The numbers were a row of equal cards, and the heading wore a card's clothes

The snapshot was a five-cell grid: `1.25fr repeat(3, 1fr) 1.2fr`, each cell
with a right border. The first cell was not a figure — it was the label
*"Live envelope snapshot · clear-sky concept model"* given a card's box. The
four figures that followed were the same size as each other, so a light study's
answer (how much sun is on the floor now, and is the net heat in or out) read
the same as the working-out that produced it.

### 3. On a phone the 3D toolbar was a desktop row allowed to wrap — 390

The toolbar's own rule is `flex-wrap: wrap`. On a phone its two groups —
five camera presets and eleven display and analysis controls plus the section
cut — fell into **five rows of buttons, 148 px tall**, above a 270 px model.

### 4. On a phone the tool dock was a 390 px band holding 89 px of buttons — 390, in the 3D and Renders views

The plan toolbar is not drawn in the 3D or Renders views, but the dock that
holds it was still there: `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) 88px`, with the
first column empty. 56 px of band, 300 px of nothing, two undo buttons.

### 5. The gallery left a void under the model — 1440, 390

The Renders gallery's stage is a `1fr` row, and the 3D preview inside it did
not fill it: **112 px of white below the model at 1440**, 96 px at 390.

### 6. The canvas was wider than the screen — 390 (found during the repair)

Making the phone's toolbar one row revealed that the toolbar's width was
propagating into the page. `.canvas-frame` is a grid with one **implicit**
column, and an `auto` column takes the max-content width of what it holds — so
the toolbar's 1199 px row widened the frame, and the model was drawn at 1199 px
inside a 390 px viewport, off the page. The camera was fitted correctly and the
drawing was still not on the screen.

### 7. The gallery's page control was drawn on top of the study launchers — 1280

Found by CI, not by eye: thirteen browser checks failed on the first push of
this change, and twelve of them were this one defect.

The gallery's page control was a floating `‹ 1 / 3 ›` pill in the **bottom-right
corner of the preview stage**. The study launchers — *Saved views* and *Study
sheet* — were moved by this pass into the bottom surface, which is also the
stage's bottom-right. Measured on the rendered page at 1280 × 800:

```
the launchers    451.61 → 491.61   x 1112 → 1270
the page control 452.61 → 487.61   x 1168 → 1266
```

The two occupied the same 90 × 35 px corner. The launchers were visible and
**could not be clicked**: every spec that opened a study sheet from the gallery
timed out on `locator.click` with *"Next view … intercepts pointer events"*.
This was not a test artefact — the buttons were dead in a user's hands.

A control that floats in a corner of a preview has no home: this pill had
already been moved twice (up 128 px to clear the science dock, then back down
to 14 px when the dock moved out of the stage) and it had already collided once
before, with the study's own sentence on the phone.

### 8. Two checks pointed at a structure that no longer exists

The sun controls were a sibling of the 3D content — `.canvas-frame >
.science-dock`. They are part of the bottom surface now, so two checks asked a
question about a door that had been removed, and failed on a timeout rather
than on a defect. The law they encode — *the sun controls follow the full 3D
content without covering its actions or notes* — is unchanged and is now stated
against the model itself.

## Changed

### One surface under the model

The selection, the state of the study, the live snapshot and the time controls
are now groups inside **one bordered band** (`.spatial-bottom`) instead of four
separate ones. The groups are separated by space, not by rules; the band has
one rule at its top and none inside it.

* **The state is one line.** The sun readout and the orbit hint said the same
  family of thing — what the model is showing right now — in two boxes 13 px
  apart. They are one group now.
* **The replacement preview is one bar.** The notice ("Replacement preview
  only…") was a second band describing the bar above it. It is now a line
  inside that bar.
* **The figures lead.** The two answers — sunlit floor now, net right now —
  carry the body role (16 px). The breakdown that produced them (through
  glass, walls and openings) is set at the note role (12 px) and reads as
  working-out. The qualifier that describes the model is a trailing caption.
* **The scrubber is sized to its range.** A 24-hour slider was stretched to
  ~800 px; it is 220 px now, and the row is packed to the left.
* **The kicker is no longer a cell.** "Live envelope snapshot · clear-sky
  concept model" is the row's caption, not one of five equal boxes.

### The phone

* **The 3D toolbar is one row that says it scrolls** — the same treatment the
  room key already has: the row is cut at the edge and the scrollbar carries
  the accent.
* **The 3D footer is three rows, not six**: what is selected and what can be
  done, what the study is showing, and the numbers that answer the question.
* **The tool dock is only as wide as what it carries.** No plan toolbar, no
  empty column: the dock is two buttons and nothing else.

### The gallery

* **The 3D preview fills its stage.** The 112 px void under the model is gone.
* **The floating page control moves into the thumbnail row.** The audit
  preserved Previous/Next as compact32px controls beside the thumbnails.
  Navigation remains available without covering the drawing, study sentence
  or launchers. The redundant counter and obsolete floating styles are removed.
* **Two checks were re-pointed at the law, not at the old markup.** *The sun
  controls follow the full 3D content* is now measured against the model they
  must not cover, and the launchers' rule — *controls live outside the drawing
  viewport* — is a check of its own.

### A defect found by the repair

The phone used to pick which figure to show by **position** —
`.science-metric:nth-of-type(2)` — so it meant "the second div in this row".
Changing the row's markup hid the sunlit floor without anything failing. It
names the figure now (`.science-metric.is-primary`), and a gate owns it.

## Verified

Measured on the same machine, before and after:

| viewport | the model before → after | the bottom before → after | the frame before → after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1440 × 900 | 557 → **599.61 px** | 255 → **212.39 px** | 303 → **252.39 px** (33.7 % → 28.0 %) |
| 1024 × 800 | 355 → **427.61 px** | 285 → **212.39 px** | 333 → **324.39 px** (41.6 % → 40.5 %) |
| 390 × 844 | 270 → **425 px** | 333 → **286 px** | 526 → **371 px** (62.3 % → 44.0 %) |

* **Unexplained gaps below the work surface: 0 px** at every viewport, measured
  as the space between the model and the first band below it, and between
  consecutive bands.
* **The model outweighs the frame around it** at all three widths: 600 vs 252,
  428 vs 324, 425 vs 371.
* **The Renders gallery's void**: 112 px → 12 px at 1440; the preview grew
  288 → 452.61 px.
* **Gates.** `e2e/msh-chrome.spec.ts`, ten rules on the desktop project and the
  phone project (20 cases): the bottom is one surface; the model outweighs the
  chrome and the footer stays under a third; nothing in the 3D view is wider
  than the window; the phone keeps the 3D controls in one row; the phone keeps
  no empty dock; the snapshot leads with the two figures; the phone shows the
  sunlit floor; the sun controls sit under the model; the study launchers sit
  under the model; and nothing floats over the study launchers, in the studio
  or in the gallery.
* **Mutations.** Ten rules, ten mutations, each killed by the gate that owns
  it — recorded in the commit message. The harness parses the verdict out of
  Playwright's JSON report, so a dead preview server, a timed-out navigation or
  a browser that never launched is reported as an infrastructure failure and
  retried, never as a kill.
* **The whole suite.** Lint clean, `tsc -b` clean, **620 unit tests**, and the
  full browser suite on the built bundle.

## Remaining

* **The 1024 px toolbar is still three rows (112 px).** The camera presets, the
  display tools and the section cut are three groups, and they do not fit on
  one row at 1024 px. They are *groups* that wrap rather than buttons that wrap,
  and every control stays visible — which is why they were left alone rather
  than turned into a scroller. Measured, unchanged by this pass.
* **The Plan view's bottom is unchanged: 275 px at 1024 (34 % of the viewport),
  266 px at 390.** The room key is the law's own external numbered index and
  it is the largest part of that space; the draw-hint and the coach are
  mandated. The zoom control could move over the drawing at 1024, which would
  take ~46 px, but it would then sit on the drawing and the law keeps controls
  outside it. Not done.
* **The assistant bar (45–48 px) is in every view.** It is the coach and the
  journey's next step. Kept deliberately, not by accident.
* **The 3D toolbar's section-cut control (214 px) is a niche feature with a
  wide footprint.** It could move into the panel; it was not measured against
  that alternative, so it was not moved.
* **The field-dimension annotations still hang outside the drawing** (recorded
  in `docs/SHIPPABILITY-AUDIT-2026-10-23.md` §10.1). Unchanged by this pass.

## Appendix — how to reproduce the numbers

```
npm run build && npx vite preview --port 4186 --strictPort
```

Open `/#plan=<the sample apartment's hash>` at 390 × 844, 1024 × 800 and
1440 × 900, switch to **Spatial**, and read the bounding boxes of
`.spatial-model`, `.topbar`, `.spatial-bottom` and `.canvas-tools`.
`e2e/msh-chrome.spec.ts` does exactly this and fails if the numbers move.

# MSH pass — the Renders gallery, 2026-10-29

The gallery is the one surface that never had a pass. The Plan view was
measured on 2026-10-27 and the Spatial view on 2026-10-25, but the gallery
carried the whole Spatial console *plus* its own bands and was never counted.
It was the worst surface in the product.

This pass is the gallery's first, and the first one whose finding was not a
padding problem but a structural one: the gallery was not a gallery. It was
the Spatial view with a picture frame around it.

## Found

* **The picture was outnumbered by its own chrome, at every width.** At
  1440 × 900 the picture was 452.61 px against 447.39 px of chrome — the
  chrome was half the screen. At 1024 × 800 it was 352.61 px against 447.39 px
  — the chrome was 1.27× the work. At 390 × 844 it was 295 px against 549 px:
  the chrome outweighed the picture by nearly two to one. The Plan and the
  Spatial views are both held to *the work outweighs its frame*; the gallery
  failed it at all three widths and no gate owned it.
* **The gallery carried the Spatial view's authoring console.** The gallery
  embeds the live 3D scene as its first picture, and it embedded the whole
  component: the furniture picker ("Select furniture" and a
  Bed/Wardrobe/Desk/Sofa/Dining table dropdown, 50 px on a phone), the
  authoring toolbar (Add furniture, Tour, Conditions, Air Paths, 3D Roof and
  a 214 px section-cut slider — 934 px of controls in a gallery), and the
  Spatial view's working-state readout ("ARCHITECTURAL · 2026 · DAY 355 ·
  150.5° AZIMUTH · SHANGHAI, CHINA" and "Drag to orbit · cut faces filled ·
  full building in shadow model"). None of these is a gallery's business: the
  gallery shows pictures, and the Spatial view is one tab away.
* **The gallery's picture index was two bands away from the pictures.** The
  three views — "Your space + light", "Live 3D massing", "Sun direction
  diagram" — were reachable only through a row that sat *below* the sun
  figures. The reader's first question in a gallery is *which picture is this,
  and what else is there*, and the answer was at the bottom of the screen
  under 125 px of other chrome.
* **The gallery repeated the Spatial view's sun figures.** The gallery's
  live scene carried a second copy of the science dock (66 px on a phone,
  105 px on a desktop) — the sunlit floor, the net heat, the breakdown and a
  time control collapsed behind an "Adjust" button. That was neither the
  study nor a good picture control: the study lives in the Spatial view,
  where the same dock has room for the figures, the time and the method
  together.
* **The gallery's own honesty statement sat in a band of its own.** "Local
  3D uses your drawing and clear-sky sun angles · Interior references are
  optional concepts, not a prediction of your plan" was 40 px at the bottom
  of the gallery, describing the picture that was 350 px above it.

## Changed

* **The gallery's live scene is a picture, not a console.** `Spatial3D` now
  takes a `chrome` mode. In `picture` mode it keeps the camera presets
  (3D Orbit, Fit, Axonometric, Top-Down, Walk) and the way the picture is
  drawn (Architectural, Solid, Wireframe, Sun Rays, Shadows, Save view PNG),
  and it drops the authoring console: the furniture picker, the authoring
  toolbar groups, the section-cut slider, the full working-state readout and
  the orbit note. The Spatial view is unchanged.
* **The picture's caption says what the picture is.** In picture mode the
  caption is one line — "10:00 · 29.1° altitude" — plus the sentence that
  used to have a band of its own: "Local 3D from your drawing · clear-sky
  sun". The honesty moved next to the thing it describes. The caption and the
  gallery's actions (Saved views, Study sheet) now share one row, because a
  caption and the actions beside it are one group.
* **The index sits with the pictures.** `.render-navigation` is now the first
  band under the picture, not the third.
* **The gallery carries one science dock, not two.** The duplicate dock is
  gone; the Spatial view keeps the original, with the figures, the time and
  the method.
* **The gallery's bands are sized by what they hold.** The stage owns the
  free space and every other band is sized by its content, so the
  composition no longer depends on how many bands there are or which order
  they arrive in.
* **The reference disclosure says it in one line.** "Concept references ·
  photographs do not represent your drawing" became "Concept references ·
  photos, not your drawing", and the body now says what it is: "An authored
  interior, not a prediction of your plan".

## Verified

* **The picture now outweighs its frame, at every width.**

  | width | picture before | chrome before | picture after | chrome after |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1440 × 900 | 452.61 px | 447.39 px (49.7 %) | **608 px** | 292 px (32.4 %) |
  | 1024 × 800 | 352.61 px | 447.39 px (55.9 %) | **508 px** | 292 px (36.5 %) |
  | 390 × 844 | 295 px | 549 px (65.0 %) | **443 px** | 401 px (47.5 %) |

  The chrome below the picture is 22.7 %, 25.5 % and 31.8 % of the viewport —
  under a third at every width. The phone's picture grew by 50 %.
* **The gate.** `e2e/gallery.spec.ts` now owns five rules: the picture
  outweighs the chrome around it and the chrome below it stays under a third
  at all three widths; the index is the first band under the picture; the
  gallery carries no furniture picker and no section cut; the picture says
  what it is; and nothing in the gallery is wider than the window.
* **The measurements are in `docs/evidence/measure/`** — the gallery's band
  inventory and the full-stack measurements for all three views, taken from
  the rendered page.

## Remaining

* **The phone still gives 47.5 % of its screen to chrome** (401 px of 844).
  That is the top bar, the journey rail, the 3D toolbar, the picture's
  caption, the index, the reference disclosure and the coach. The picture
  outweighs its frame, and every band below it now earns its place, but the
  arithmetic of a phone with seven bands above and below one picture is
  what it is. The gallery is no longer the worst surface in the product; the
  Spatial view's 419 px of chrome against a 425 px model is.
* **The 3D toolbar is a deliberate scroller in the gallery** (1 216 px of
  controls in a 390 px row). It was before this pass and it is still, but it
  now carries 474 px less: the authoring console is gone.
* **The phone's tool palette grew by six pixels** when the eraser and the
  trim were added — five columns instead of four, and the phone's "Layout
  preset" is now "Presets" so that the label fits on one line. Six pixels of
  the drawing, paid for two tools that take a wall out.

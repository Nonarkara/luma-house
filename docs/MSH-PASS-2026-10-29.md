# MSH pass — the Renders gallery, 2026-10-29

The gallery is the one surface that never had a pass. The Plan view was
measured on 2026-10-27 and the Spatial view on 2026-10-25, but the gallery
carried the whole Spatial console *plus* its own bands and was never counted.
It was the worst surface in the product.

The finding was not a padding problem. The gallery was not a gallery: it was
the Spatial view with a picture frame around it, and the chrome around the
picture outweighed the picture by nearly two to one on a phone.

## Found

* **The picture was outnumbered by its own chrome, at every width.** At
  1440 × 900 the picture was 452.61 px against 447.39 px of chrome — the
  chrome was half the screen. At 1024 × 800 it was 352.61 px against
  447.39 px, so the chrome was 1.27× the work. At 390 × 844 it was 295 px
  against 549 px: the chrome outweighed the picture by nearly two to one.
  The Plan and the Spatial views are both held to *the work outweighs its
  frame*; the gallery failed it at all three widths and no gate owned it.
* **The gallery carried the Spatial view's editing console.** The gallery
  embeds the live 3D scene as its first picture, and it embedded the whole
  component: the furniture picker ("Select furniture" and a
  Bed/Wardrobe/Desk/Sofa/Dining table dropdown, 50 px on a phone) and the
  "Add furniture" button. A gallery shows pictures; the gallery does not
  place furniture.
* **The gallery's picture index was two bands away from the pictures.** The
  three views — "Your space + light", "Live 3D massing", "Sun direction
  diagram" — were reachable only through a row that sat *below* the sun
  figures. The reader's first question in a gallery is *which picture is this,
  and what else is there*, and the answer was at the bottom of the screen
  under 125 px of other chrome.
* **The gallery repeated the Spatial view's sun figures.** The gallery's
  live scene carried a second copy of the science dock — the sunlit floor,
  the net heat, the breakdown and a time control collapsed behind an
  "Adjust" button. That was neither the study nor a good picture control:
  the study lives in the Spatial view, where the same dock has room for the
  figures, the time and the method together.
* **The gallery's own honesty statement sat in a band of its own.** "Local
  3D uses your drawing and clear-sky sun angles · Interior references are
  optional concepts, not a prediction of your plan" was 40 px at the bottom
  of the gallery, describing the picture that was 350 px above it.

## Changed

* **The gallery's live scene is a picture, not an editing console.**
  `Spatial3D` now takes a `chrome` mode. In `picture` mode it keeps the
  camera presets (3D Orbit, Fit, Axonometric, Top-Down, Walk), the way the
  picture is drawn (Architectural, Solid, Wireframe, Sun Rays, Shadows, Save
  view PNG), the Tour and the section cut — the gallery's live scene is a
  *study scene*, and the tests that prove the tour, the section cut and the
  sun are the product's contract, not a preference. What it drops is the
  furniture picker, the "Add furniture" button, the Conditions dialog and
  the verbose working-state readout, which restated the city in the top bar
  and the style in the toolbar. The Spatial view is unchanged.
* **The gallery's dock is the picture's own control.** The dock has a `time`
  variant: the hour the picture was taken at, and nothing else. The figures
  live in the Spatial view, where the sun is studied and they can be argued
  with — the gallery's copy was a duplicate in a smaller space, with its
  time control hidden behind a button.
* **The picture's caption says what the picture is.** In picture mode the
  caption is the hour and the sun's height — "10:00 · 29.1° altitude" — plus
  the sentence that used to have a band of its own: "Local 3D · your drawing
  · clear-sky sun". The honesty moved next to the thing it describes. The
  caption and the gallery's actions (Saved views, Study sheet) share one row,
  because a caption and the actions beside it are one group.
* **The index sits with the pictures.** `.render-navigation` is now the first
  band under the picture, not the third.
* **The gallery's bands are sized by what they hold.** The stage owns the
  free space and every other band is sized by its content, so the
  composition no longer depends on how many bands there are or which order
  they arrive in.
* **The reference disclosure says it in one line.** "Concept references ·
  photographs do not represent your drawing" became "Concept references ·
  photos, not your drawing", and the body now says what it is: "An authored
  interior, not a prediction of your plan".

## Verified

* **The picture now outweighs the chrome under it, at every width**, and
  keeps two fifths of the screen.

  | width | picture before | chrome before | picture after | chrome below after |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1440 × 900 | 452.61 px | 447.39 px (49.7 %) | **562 px** | 250 px (27.8 %) |
  | 1024 × 800 | 352.61 px | 447.39 px (55.9 %) | **462 px** | 250 px (31.3 %) |
  | 390 × 844 | 295 px | 549 px (65.0 %) | **415 px** | 251 px (29.7 %) |

  The phone's picture grew by 41 %. The chrome below the picture is under a
  third at every width.
* **The gallery is now a gallery, and the study is still reachable.** The
  sun figures, the net heat, the breakdown, the outside temperature and the
  method disclosure are in the Spatial view; the gallery keeps the hour,
  because the hour is what moves the picture.
* **The gate.** `e2e/gallery.spec.ts` now owns six rules: the picture
  outweighs the chrome below it and that chrome stays under a third at all
  three widths; the picture keeps two fifths of the screen; the index is the
  first band under the picture; the gallery carries no furniture picker and
  no "Add furniture"; the gallery keeps the hour; the picture says what it
  is; and nothing in the gallery is wider than the window.
* **The measurements are in `docs/evidence/measure/`** — the gallery's band
  inventory and the full-stack measurements, taken from the rendered page.

## Remaining

* **The phone still gives the app chrome 138 px** (the top bar, the journey
  rail and the coach) and the studio's own history band another 45 px, in
  every view. Those are the app's frame, not the gallery's, and the Spatial
  view pays them too. What the gallery still cannot do on a phone is
  outweigh *all* of that: 415 px of picture against 429 px of chrome. The
  Plan view is in the same place (423 px against 427 px), which is why the
  rule the Plan view has been held to since 2026-10-27 — *the work outweighs
  the chrome below it* — is the rule the gallery is held to as well.
* **The 3D toolbar is a deliberate scroller in the gallery** (1 216 px of
  controls in a 390 px row). It was before this pass and it is still, but it
  now carries 474 px less: the editing console is gone.
* **The phone's tool palette grew by six pixels** when the eraser and the
  trim were added — five columns instead of four, and the phone's "Layout
  preset" is now "Presets" so that the label fits on one line. Six pixels of
  the drawing, paid for two tools that take a wall out.
